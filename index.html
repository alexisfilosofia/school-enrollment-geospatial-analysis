School Enrollment and Geospatial Analysis

A notebook-derived, privacy-aware analytical project based on historical school enrollment records from 1910 to 1915. The project transforms fragmented institutional spreadsheets into aggregate quantitative, temporal and geospatial outputs.

Read project documentation Notebook output audit

Overview

From Colab notebooks to a public portfolio report

Problem

Historical school records are often distributed across spreadsheets with heterogeneous labels, date formats, course names and sensitive personal information. The challenge is to produce meaningful analysis without publishing student-level data.

Approach

The project reorganizes outputs from two Colab notebooks into a public web report. It keeps aggregate findings, selected charts and methodological notes, while excluding raw names, addresses and row-level records.

Notebook sources

What was taken from the original Colab work

The public site is based on the outputs and printed summaries from the original Colab notebooks. Sensitive raw data and row-level tables are intentionally not included.

Archivo_PST_Cuanti.ipynb

Consolidates enrollment spreadsheets, normalizes course labels, reconstructs dates and produces quantitative outputs: annual enrollment, age statistics, age ranges, course-year matrices and interannual course variation.

Archivo_PST_GEO_Final.ipynb

Builds the geospatial layer: distance-to-school calculations, geocoded case summaries, occupation groups, nationality distributions and export decisions for maps and public-safe reporting.

Quantitative Analysis

Enrollment volume, age structure and course-level dynamics

These outputs are derived from the quantitative notebook and converted into public-safe aggregate views.

Annual enrollment growth

The consolidated dataset grows from 155 records in 1910 to 355 in 1915. The strongest increase appears after 1913, with a marked rise in 1914 and 1915.

Notebook source: annual count table and bar chart from Archivo_PST_Cuanti.ipynb.

Age composition

The notebook groups ages into interpretable bands: 13, 14–15, 16–17, 18–24 and 25+. This supports a reading of the enrollment population beyond averages alone.

Age dispersion and outliers

The median age is stable at 18 from 1910 to 1914 and falls to 17 in 1915. The maximum value reaches 37 in 1915, treated as an outlier requiring contextual review rather than automatic deletion.

Course-year matrix

The course-level matrix shows that growth is concentrated in the lower entry courses. Between 1910 and 1915, 1º Año grows from 44 to 139 records, while 2º Año grows from 22 to 78 records.

Geospatial Analysis

Territorial reach and aggregate socio-spatial indicators

The geospatial notebook is represented through aggregate summaries only. Exact addresses, raw coordinates and individual point maps are excluded from the public version.

Distance-to-school distribution

The geospatial notebook reports 323 geocoded cases. The approximate mean distance to school is 3.12 km, while the median distance is 2.34 km.

Guardian nationality

Guardian nationality is more heterogeneous than student nationality: Argentine, Italian, missing/undetermined, Spanish and French are the most frequent categories in the notebook summary.

Ethical and Privacy-Aware Publication

Why the public version is selective

No raw student data

The original datasets are excluded because they may include names, addresses, family information and institutional records.

Aggregate outputs only

The web page shows aggregate charts, summary tables and notebook-derived findings rather than row-level tables.

Spatial caution

Exact point maps and sensitive demographic categories are not crossed publicly in order to reduce re-identification risk.
